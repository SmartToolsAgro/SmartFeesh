# Open questions for the client

Never assume an answer. When a question is answered, write the answer and the date, then update any
provisional decision in `DECISIONS.md` that depends on it.

Use `prototype/index.html` during the workshop: clients answer more precisely when they point at a screen.

Answers dated 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10 were given by Mahmoud in a working session. Items marked
**To confirm** are details still missing; ask them at the client workshop (P0-T01).

| ID | Question | Blocks | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | **Profit basis.** Does each partner supplier get a % of the net profit of **their own products**, a % of **total company** profit, or does it differ per supplier? | Phase 6 | **Own products only** (2026-10-09). Each supplier gets a % of the net profit made on the products they supplied. |
| Q2 | **Expense allocation.** How are shared costs (fuel, salaries, electricity…) split between suppliers: by sales, by gross profit, by nature of the cost, or decided by the owner each month? Is it written in the contracts? | P6-T04 | **By nature of the cost** (2026-10-09, detailed 2026-10-10). Storage costs (electricity, cold room) by kg held in stock; fuel, salaries and administration by sales. The rules are a verbal agreement with the suppliers, not written in the contracts. |
| Q3 | **Investor capital.** When a supplier invests money, is the capital paid back separately from the profit share? When? What happens in a month with a loss? | P6-T02, P6-T07 | **Repaid through the profit share** (2026-10-09): monthly payments first repay the capital, then count as profit once it is fully repaid. **Loss month: the supplier carries their % of the loss, deducted from their capital** (2026-10-10). **Two rates per partner** (2026-10-10): one % applied in a profit month, another % in a loss month; they may differ. **Provisional (D-031):** the part of a loss share that the remaining capital cannot cover, or the whole loss share of a partner without capital, is carried as an amount the partner owes and is recovered from their next profit shares. **To confirm with the client.** |
| Q4 | **Same product, several suppliers.** Can Crevette Royale come from two suppliers at different prices in the same month? | D-009, P1-T05 | **Yes, tracked separately** (2026-10-09). Each supplier's batches stay separate so profit goes to the right supplier. |
| Q5 | **Invoices and tax.** VAT rates on each product? Fiscal stamp amount? Is the business required to use TTN / El Fatoora e-invoicing? Withholding tax (retenue à la source) with some customers? | P2-T05 | (2026-10-09, detailed 2026-10-10) **VAT:** one rate for all products, 19 %. **Prices:** whether sale prices include VAT is a company setting. **Fiscal stamp:** 1.000 DT per invoice. **Withholding tax:** yes with some customers; optional per invoice, chosen before printing; the rate is editable in the finance settings and when it is 0 % nothing is shown on the invoice; tracked in the finance module. **TTN / El Fatoora:** required now, but **not implemented for now** (2026-10-10, D-016). **To confirm with the accountant:** the VAT code lists Tunisian fishing products as exempt, which does not match 19 % for all; and e-invoicing has been mandatory for VAT-registered businesses since 01/01/2026 (see `docs/spec/sample/README.md`). |
| Q6 | **Payroll.** How many employees? Monthly fixed salary, daily pay, or commission? Which employer contributions (CNSS) must be tracked? | P5-T03 | **Fixed monthly salary; employer CNSS contributions are tracked** as a monthly expense; **6 to 10 employees** (2026-10-10). |
| Q7 | **Costing.** Is a sold kilo valued at its own batch cost (FIFO) or at an average cost? Does the accountant have a preference? | D-009, P2-T04 | **Its own batch cost, oldest batch first (FIFO)** (2026-10-09). Purchase prices change from one day to the next (2026-10-10), so each receipt sets the cost of its own batch. |
| Q8 | **Locations.** How many cold rooms / freezers and trucks? Does the owner also sell over the counter at the cold room? | P1-T04 | **2 cold rooms and 3 or more trucks; over-the-counter sales at the cold room happen regularly** (2026-10-09, detailed 2026-10-10). The exact truck count comes with the client's truck list. |
| Q9 | **Month-end.** Who approves the closing? Is profit calculated on sales made (accrual) or on cash received? | P6-T06 | **Profit on sales made (accrual). The accountant checks the closing in the app with their own login, then the owner approves** (2026-10-10). |
| Q10 | **Units.** Which products are sold by the box or piece instead of by kg? Box weights? | P1-T01 | **Everything is sold by kg** (2026-10-09). |
| Q11 | **Mixed products.** What goes into "Symphonie Marine"? If it mixes several suppliers' products, who gets the profit? | P1-T05, P6-T05 | **The mix always comes from a single supplier** (2026-10-10), so it is shared like any other product. |
| Q12 | **Weight loss.** Frozen products with ice glaze, thawing, drying: what loss is normal? Is weight on supplier invoices gross or net? | P1-T07 | **Supplier invoices show net weight**; later loss is small and exceptional (2026-10-09). |
| Q13 | **Drivers' phones.** Android or iPhone? Is mobile data reliable on all routes? Do they need to print a receipt (Bluetooth printer)? | Phase 3 | (2026-10-09, detailed 2026-10-10) **Android and iPhone.** **Some dead zones** on the routes: the app must work offline and sync later. **Receipts: both printed (Bluetooth printer) and digital, in French.** No printer chosen yet: to pick in the P3-T10 spike. |
| Q14 | **Language.** Do drivers prefer French or Arabic on their phone? | P3 UI | **Both; each driver chooses** French or Arabic (2026-10-09). |
| Q15 | **Existing data.** Where are customers, suppliers, products, debts and stock kept today (Excel, notebook, other software)? | P4-T01 | **Paper notebooks** (2026-10-09). Data will have to be typed in. |
| Q16 | **Customer credit.** Credit limits? Who may sell on credit: every driver or only the owner? | P2-T06 | **Every driver, up to a credit limit set per customer** (2026-10-09). What happens above the limit (block, ask for approval, or warn) is a **company setting** (2026-10-10). |
| Q17 | **Future.** Could this software one day be sold to other distributors? (We are ready for it technically; this changes priorities, not code.) | Phase 8 | **Yes, it is a goal** (2026-10-10). |
| Q18 | **TTN enrolment.** Is the company already registered with TTN / El Fatoora? Does it have the electronic signing certificate? How are invoices sent to TTN today, and who holds the technical documentation? | Phase 8 | _on hold_ · TTN is not implemented for now (2026-10-10, D-016). Ask again when e-invoicing is picked from the Phase 8 backlog. |
