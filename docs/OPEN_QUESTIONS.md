# Open questions for the client

Never assume an answer. When a question is answered, write the answer and the date, then update any
provisional decision in `DECISIONS.md` that depends on it.

Use `prototype/index.html` during the workshop: clients answer more precisely when they point at a screen.

| ID | Question | Blocks | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | **Profit basis.** Does each partner supplier get a % of the net profit of **their own products**, a % of **total company** profit, or does it differ per supplier? | Phase 6 | _open_ |
| Q2 | **Expense allocation.** How are shared costs (fuel, salaries, electricity…) split between suppliers: by sales, by gross profit, by nature of the cost, or decided by the owner each month? Is it written in the contracts? | P6-T04 | _open_ |
| Q3 | **Investor capital.** When a supplier invests money, is the capital paid back separately from the profit share? When? What happens in a month with a loss? | P6-T02, P6-T07 | _open_ |

| Q5 | **Invoices and tax.** VAT rates on each product? Fiscal stamp amount? Is the business required to use TTN / El Fatoora e-invoicing? Withholding tax (retenue à la source) with some customers? | P2-T05 | _open_ |
| Q6 | **Payroll.** How many employees? Monthly fixed salary, daily pay, or commission? Which employer contributions (CNSS) must be tracked? | P5-T03 | _open_ |
